Lyrics in a pill.

Verse floats a slim glass pill above everything on your Mac and sings the current line of whatever's playing. Click it, and it unfurls into a karaoke card.

Download for Mac or install from the terminal

Free and open source. macOS 14 or later, any Mac.

Download for Mac
From the terminal — skips the Gatekeeper warning:
$ brew install --cask cpt-nem0/tap/verse
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cpt-nem0/verse/main/install.sh | bash

The direct download is ad-hoc signed: right-click the app and choose Open the first time you launch it.
